⚡ 5X Intraday Margin Leverage, Opening Gap & 1st 5m Candle ORB Rules

• 5X Intraday Leverage (MIS): Each ₹10,000 allocated capital provides ₹50,000 in buying power / trade exposure (20% margin requirement).
• Opening Price Alignment (Prev Close Check):
  – BUY Setups: Price must open ≥ Previous Day's Close (confirms buyer aggression).
  – SELL Setups: Price must open ≤ Previous Day's Close (confirms supply pressure).
• Gap Exhaustion Rule (> 2.5%): Extreme runaway gaps (> +2.5% gap-up for Buys or < -2.5% gap-down for Sells) are disqualified due to exhaustion and instant mean-reversion risk.
• Intraday ORB Trigger: For Buys, enter strictly on break of 1st 5m candle High (SL = Day's Low). For Sells, enter on break of 1st 5m candle Low (SL = Day's High).
• Erratic 2% Filter: If the 1st 5-minute candle range exceeds 2.0% of the stock price, the stock is automatically disqualified to avoid opening whip-saws.
